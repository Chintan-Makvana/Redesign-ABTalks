WHAT ABTALKS IS

A place to practice in public, not just talk about it.

ABTalks is a community and platform for students, developers, and AI enthusiasts who want to build a real coding habit — one shipped project at a time — instead of collecting another set of half-finished tutorials.

The hackathon is where that habit gets tested: a 48-hour sprint to design, build, and deploy a working AI-powered project, solo or with up to two teammates, using whichever modern AI tool fits your workflow.

Every submission becomes part of your public portfolio — your proof of work — so recruiters see what you can actually ship, not just what you say you can do.

THE 48 HOURS

How the sprint actually unfolds.

A real sequence, so treat it like one — this is the order the dashboard will guide you through.

T−0

Kickoff & team lock-in

HOUR 0

Challenge briefs go live on the dashboard. Finalize your team of up to three, or confirm you're going solo.

T+2

Build with your AI tool of choice

HOURS 2–40

Design, code, and deploy using Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Cursor, Windsurf, Bolt, Replit, Lovable, GitHub Copilot, or any accepted tool.

T+40

Docs & demo video

HOURS 40–46

Record how you used AI tools and why — this is part of the rubric, not an afterthought.

T+48

Submission deadline

HOUR 48

Submit your live demo link, GitHub repo, and documentation through the dashboard's submission gateway.

T+72

Judging, leaderboard & certificates

FEW DAYS AFTER

Standings post to the leaderboard, certificates unlock, and shortlisted teams hear about hiring and internship opportunities.
